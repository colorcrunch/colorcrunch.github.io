HOW TO PLAY COLORCRUNCH

Welcome to ColorCrunch! A colourful block puzzle game where strategy, smart moves and big combos help you achieve the highest score possible!

1. Place Your Blocks

You'll be given three blocks or block shapes to choose from. Drag and drop them onto the 10×10 game board. Think carefully about where you place them, because every move counts!

2. Match Colours

Place blocks of the same colour together to create matching groups. When enough matching blocks connect, they'll disappear from the board, freeing up space and earning you points. The more blocks you clear at once, the more points you'll earn!
